The idea

Cozy Cafe is a scan-to-order experience designed for dine-in cafes. Customers scan a QR code on the table, browse the menu, add items, and place their order — no app download, no waving for a waiter, no friction. The kitchen gets the order instantly, and guests track their items in real time right from the table.

Key features

Designed around the guest experience

01
QR-first access
No app download. Guests scan the table QR and the menu loads instantly in the browser.
02
Live order tracking
Each item shows a real-time status — Preparing, Ready, or Served — so guests always know what's coming.
03
Category browsing
Coffee, Snacks, Bakery and more. Items are tagged as Best Seller, Popular, New, or Classic.
04
Cart & bill summary
Promo code support, GST breakdown, and a clear total before the order is confirmed.
05
Finalise & call waiter
Once done, guests tap to lock the bill and summon the waiter to their table.
06
Session-based ordering
Tied to a table number and session, guests can keep adding items anytime during their visit.
