The idea

Cozy Cafe is a scan-to-order experience designed for dine-in cafes. Customers scan a QR code on the table, browse the menu, add items, and place their order — no app download, no waving for a waiter, no friction. The kitchen gets the order instantly, and guests track their items in real time right from the table.

User Flow

The flow

Scan · Browse · Order · Track

01
Step One
Menu view
After scanning the table QR, guests land on a warm, category-driven menu. No login required.
02
Step Two
Adding items
Guests browse, tap, and customise — adding items to cart with a single tap, no confusion.
03
Step Three
Cart review
Full bill summary with promo codes, GST breakdown, and a clear total before confirming.
04
Step Four
Order tracking
Each item shows live status — Preparing, Ready, Served. Guests always know what's coming.
