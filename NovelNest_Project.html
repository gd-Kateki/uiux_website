UX Challenges Addressed
1
Content Discovery
Genre tiles + hero banner + chip filters let Aarav find a story in under 3 taps.
2
Publishing Friction
3-step novel form + dual input for chapters + auto-save removes all key blockers for Sarah.
3
Reader Retention
Progress bars, "2 new chapters" badges, achievements, and reading streaks drive Aarav back daily.
4
Reader–Author Interaction
Feedback screen with reply CTA + "unanswered" count creates a meaningful loop between creators and fans.
